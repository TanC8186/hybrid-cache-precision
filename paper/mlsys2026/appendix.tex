\section{Byte-exact parameter derivation}
\label{app:bytes}

The attention term includes all GQA layers. At fp16, one attention layer uses
two tensors (K and V), 512 elements per token, and 2 bytes per element. This
is 2,048 bytes per layer and token. At int4, K and V each use 256 packed
payload bytes plus 8 metadata bytes. The total is therefore
$2\times(256+8)=528$ bytes per layer and token.

Each GDN layer contains two state components. The convolution state is bf16
in both configurations and uses 36,864 bytes. The temporal state uses
1,048,576 bytes at fp32 or 524,288 bytes at bf16. Consequently,
\begin{align}
G^{\mathrm{layer}}_{\mathrm{fp32}} &= 36{,}864+1{,}048{,}576
 = 1{,}085{,}440,\\
G^{\mathrm{layer}}_{\mathrm{bf16}} &= 36{,}864+524{,}288
 = 561{,}152.
\end{align}
The complete layer state falls by 48.30\%, not 50\%.

\section{Discrete allocator reconstruction}
\label{app:discrete}

Let $K$ be the GPU-block count, $H$ the recurrent cache-group count, $B$ the
attention block size, and $L$ the requested context. The allocator-equivalent
slot count and token capacity are
\begin{equation}
S_{\mathrm{alloc}}=\frac{K}{H+\lceil L/B\rceil},\qquad
T_{\mathrm{alloc}}=\lfloor L S_{\mathrm{alloc}}\rfloor.
\end{equation}

For 2B/int4/4K with fp32 state, $K=3287$, $H=3$, and $B=2064$.
The denominator is $3+\lceil4096/2064\rceil=5$. Thus
$S_{\mathrm{alloc}}=657.4$ and $T_{\mathrm{alloc}}=2{,}692{,}710$.
With bf16 state, $K=6330$ and $B=1072$. The denominator is seven,
$S_{\mathrm{alloc}}=904.286$, and
$T_{\mathrm{alloc}}=3{,}703{,}954$.

Fractional slot counts are an allocator normalization. They do not mean that
the scheduler admits a fractional request, nor do they prove admission of the
integer floor under prefill, decode, or latency constraints.

\section{Full-matrix and temporal checks}
\label{app:matrix}

The formal matrix contains 112 allocator cells and 52 matched fp32/bf16 core
pairs. Every pair favors bf16 state. The median capacity increase is 15.44\%,
with a range of 3.32--93.28\%. The corrected continuous-model residual has
2.3795\% median absolute magnitude, 2.7955\% mean absolute magnitude, and
13.2134\% maximum absolute magnitude. Signed residuals span
$-5.1604\%$ to $+13.2134\%$.

The temporal rerun repeats the frozen matrix. The allocator is deterministic
for a fixed build and configuration, so this is a reproducibility check rather
than an independent statistical replicate. All cell directions agree and the
maximum token difference is 1.42\%.

\section{Dependence-aware GSM8K analysis}
\label{app:gsm8k}

Each dataset seed samples 200 GSM8K items without replacement. Samples overlap
across seeds, producing 1,800 seed-item draws but only 1,017 unique items.
Pairwise seed overlap ranges from 19 to 39, with median 30. The primary
estimand is the mean paired difference over the observed seed-item draws.

\begin{figure*}[t]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.72\textwidth]{figures/vector_redesign/fig8_gsm8k_per_seed.pdf}
\caption{\textbf{Descriptive GSM8K dataset-seed summaries.} Thin lines show
nine seed summaries. The blue line is the draw-weighted mean. These lines are
not independent replicates and are not the basis of the confidence intervals.}
\label{fig:seed-summaries}
\end{figure*}

For each candidate, an intercept-only OLS model is fit to paired binary
differences. The covariance uses two-way CR1 clustering by item and dataset
seed. The reference distribution is Student-$t$ with
$\min(1017-1,9-1)=8$ degrees of freedom. A percentile bootstrap samples the
1,017 item-level means with equal item weight for 20,000 replicates.

Repeated items are not always deterministic across dataset seeds despite
greedy decoding. The 2B full, state-only, KV-only, and joint allocations have
30, 27, 47, and 43 repeated items with inconsistent outcomes. Clustering
accounts for dependence but does not explain this behavior. The old script
that treated seed means as iid now exits with a deprecation error.

\section{Artifact and verification manifest}
\label{app:artifact}

repository-local verifier checks SHA-256 digests for 16 frozen sources,
asserts headline values and all 52 paired capacity rows, and rejects old
capacity and seed-iid GSM8K inputs. It also checks all eight SVG/PDF pairs for
nonempty vector output. The selector audit generator verifies the four
decision sidecars before producing its JSON and LaTeX tables.
