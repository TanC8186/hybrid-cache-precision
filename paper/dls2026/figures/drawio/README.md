# Draw.io source package

This directory contains editable system and mechanism schematics created and
reviewed in Next AI Draw.io. Quantitative panels remain generated from frozen
repository artifacts by the Python figure script.

Expected source/export set:

- `fig1_hybrid_allocator.drawio`, `.svg`, and `.pdf`
- `fig2_discrete_allocator.drawio`, `.svg`, and `.pdf`

The Draw.io source font floors are 14 pt for Figure 1 and 12 pt for Figure 2;
both remain at least 5 pt after their final MLSys two-column scaling.

The prompts are retained as an audit trail. They explicitly prohibit claims of
scheduler concurrency, SLO admission, independent replication, or stable
serving benefit.
