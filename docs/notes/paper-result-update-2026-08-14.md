## Review issues closed by this update

- Capacity notation now distinguishes concurrent sequences `N(L)` and total
  token capacity `T(L)`.
- All `conservative lower bound` language is removed. The model is described
  through measured residuals.
- Attention KV and recurrent state are consistently described as additive
  consumers of the same GPU memory pool.
- The executable selector, inputs, constraints, objective, cold-restart
  mapping, and verified scope are stated in the manuscript.
- RULER uses the no-think protocol; the default-thinking prose and appendix
  grid are removed, and Figure 3 uses the verified five-cell result.
- M3 fixed-block-count, fixed-bytes, and fixed-concurrency results replace the
  earlier statement that controlled mechanism contrasts were not run.
- M4 is disclosed as execution-complete but not reproducible under its frozen
  continuous-goodput gate.
- The setup no longer assigns one vLLM commit or memory-utilization value to
  every result. It distinguishes the earlier `55f4768` packages from the
  later `e2fa285` packages and scopes utilization to each frozen protocol.

## Remaining reviewer risks

- Only one GPU family and one hybrid model family are measured; TP=2/4 and a
  second hardware platform remain untested.
- The controller is profile-driven and verified on one narrow serving slice;
  online adaptation and transition overhead remain unmeasured.
- No external KV offloading, prefix-caching, or alternative state-compression
  baseline is included.
- RULER has only three paired dataset seeds and 20 samples per cell. Exact
  agreement must not be rewritten as equivalence.
- M3 latency and M4 continuous goodput failed their promotion gates. Those
  failures are evidence boundaries, not results to omit.

## Figure 3 contract

  allocations have identical observed accuracy across three paired dataset
